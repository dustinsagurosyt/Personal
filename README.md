# Attention Reset

A simple iPhone home-screen tracker, hosted with GitHub Pages. No extra account, server, installation tools, or notification setup.

## Daily use

- Aim for 60 minutes or less of short-form video. The app records your entry; it does not measure or block other apps.
- Park your phone and do one 20-minute phone-free focus session. The timer is silent.
- Capture thoughts you want to remember.
- Take a two-minute evening check-in.
- Review seven days of visible progress. After a slip, close the feed, park the phone, and take one two-minute step. Your progress stays.

## Publish once

1. Open [this repository’s Pages settings](https://github.com/dustinsagurosyt/Personal/settings/pages).
2. Under Source, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Choose **main** and **/(root)**.
4. Click **Save**, then wait for GitHub to show the published address.

The expected address after publication is https://dustinsagurosyt.github.io/Personal/ . GitHub Pages must be enabled before it works.

## Add to iPhone

Open the published address in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, turn on Open as Web App if shown, then tap Add. Open it from its home-screen icon each day.

## Your data

Notes and progress save only in the browser and device you use. They are not uploaded to GitHub. Clearing browser data removes them; use Download my record for a backup. Records from a previous app address do not move automatically.

The published app has a public address, but personal entries stay on your device. This version does not send notifications.
