The 7-day starter plan

Every day: ≤60 minutes of short-form video, one 20-minute phone-free session, phone out of reach, and one thought captured. Aim for consistency; use the 2-minute restart when needed.

  1. Set the scene. Choose one phone parking spot. Set a combined 60-minute limit across short-video apps using your phone’s settings. Move feed apps off the home screen.
  2. Make starting easy. Before 9 am, put one task and the materials you need within reach.
  3. Give scrolling a boundary. Choose two 30-minute windows within the 60-minute total; turn off nonessential feed notifications.
  4. Catch the thought. Keep a paper slip beside your work. Write the thought; return to your task.
  5. Notice one trigger. When you reach for a feed, name what you need: rest, company, or stimulation. Try a short walk, stretch, or message.
  6. Practice the return. If you scroll longer than planned, close it and take one 2-minute step. Keep your original record.
  7. Keep what works. Review the week. Keep the same daily targets and change one source of friction for next week.
